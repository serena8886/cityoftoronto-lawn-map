      Method
        Limitations
        • Land cover is from 2018; some lots will have changed since.
        • The data is a top-down view: lawn under tree canopy is counted as tree.
        • The 2018 documentation notes some confusion between tree and grass/shrub in the automated classification.
        • Only lots covered by Zoning By-law 569-2013 are included; areas still under former municipal by-laws are under-counted.
        • The map shows where lawns are, not who maintains them. In areas where landscaping companies do yard work, messaging may need to target them (e.g. “ask your landscaper to leave the leaves in garden beds”).
